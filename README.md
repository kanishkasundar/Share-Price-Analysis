# Share-Price-Analysis\

Reliance Industries: Its share price is supported by strong performance in oil, retail, and Jio telecom businesses.

Company observation: Diversified operations provide growth opportunities, though large investments can affect profits.

Tata Consultancy Services: Its share price remains relatively stable because of steady global demand for IT services.

Company observation: TCS has strong client relationships, skilled employees, and consistent profitability.

HDFC Bank: Share price movement is influenced by loan growth, interest rates, and overall banking conditions.

Company observation: It has a large customer base and strong banking services across India.

Infosys: Its share price depends on global IT spending and demand for digital transformation services.

Company observation: Infosys is known for innovation, global presence, and strong technology expertise.

Bharti Airtel: Its share price is driven by subscriber growth, data usage, and telecom competition.

Company observation: Airtel has a strong network and benefits from growing demand for mobile internet services.
